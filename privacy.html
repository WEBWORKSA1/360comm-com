Privacy Policy

Effective Sep 30, 2026.

Information we collect

Information you submit in forms (such as name, business name, email, phone, postal code and requirements), and technical data such as device, browser, pages visited and cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries and deliver requested quotes.
  • With your consent, to share quote requests with up to five relevant providers or authorized resellers who may contact you.
  • To send newsletters you subscribe to (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To operate, secure and improve the site, and to show advertising.

SMS / text messaging

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties for their marketing purposes, except with the partners you authorize for your quote request. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Cookies & advertising

We and third parties such as Google may use cookies to serve and measure ads based on prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Ads Settings and manage cookies in your browser. Our cookie banner lets you choose essential-only cookies.

Form processing

Forms are transmitted securely to our inbox through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). Donations are processed by third-party payment platforms (e.g., PayPal) under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA/Quebec Law 25), you may request access, correction or deletion of your data. Use the contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Children

This site is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 16.
