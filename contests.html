Official rules (summary)

Who can enter?

Legal residents 18+ of eligible countries where not prohibited by law. Employees of sponsors and their immediate families are not eligible. Void where prohibited.

Is a purchase required?

No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win. A purchase does not improve your chances.

How are winners chosen?

Skill-based judging by the 360Comm editorial panel using published criteria: originality (30%), usefulness to other businesses (40%), clarity (30%).

How are prizes delivered?

Winners are notified by email within 14 days of the close date and must respond within 14 days. Prizes are delivered electronically (gift card/transfer) or shipped. Taxes are the winner's responsibility.

How is my entry used?

Winning and shortlisted entries may be published on 360Comm with credit. You keep ownership of your content and grant us a non-exclusive license to display it.
