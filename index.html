Frequently asked questions

Is 360Comm free to use?

Yes. Comparisons, guides, calculators and quote matching are free for businesses. We earn revenue from advertising, sponsorships, affiliate partnerships and vendor referral fees — see How We Make Money.

How does the free quote service work?

You answer a few questions about users, needs and timeline. We share your request with up to five relevant providers or authorized resellers, who contact you with tailored pricing. There is no obligation to buy.

How much does a business VoIP system cost?

Most small-business cloud phone plans start between about $10 and $30 per user per month on annual billing, before taxes and regulatory fees that commonly add 15–25%.

How do you score providers?

Our 360 Score weighs pricing & value, features, reliability & security, ease of use, support and third-party user sentiment. Full weights are on our methodology page.

Can I advertise or sponsor 360Comm?

Yes — sponsored listings, newsletter placements, contests and category sponsorships are available. Paid placements are always labeled.
