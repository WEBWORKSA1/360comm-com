What to look for

  • Message history and search limits on free plans
  • Guest/external collaboration (shared channels)
  • Integrations and workflow automation
  • Huddles / quick calls
  • Data retention and eDiscovery
  • SSO and security controls
  • Mobile app quality
  • Pricing per active user

Typical pricing

Common business plans run about $4–$15 per user per month; many teams get chat bundled in their UCaaS or productivity suite.
