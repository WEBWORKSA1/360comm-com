Business Internet & Connectivity

Every cloud communications tool depends on the connection underneath it. Business internet options include cable, fiber, dedicated internet access (DIA), fixed wireless and 5G/LTE backup, often combined with SD-WAN for failover.

Top options

Business cable

Business fiber

Dedicated fiber (DIA)

Uncontended bandwidth with SLAs

Fixed wireless

Fast install where fiber is absent

5G / LTE backup

Automatic failover for uptime

SD-WAN

Bonds multiple links; prioritizes voice/video

What to look for

  • Symmetrical upload speed (critical for video)
  • Service-level agreement and repair times
  • Static IP availability
  • Failover / backup (5G, second ISP)
  • Latency and jitter under load
  • Contract length and installation fees
  • QoS support on your router
  • Local availability by address

Typical pricing

Small-business cable/fiber plans commonly run about $50–$250 per month; dedicated fiber (DIA) typically runs several hundred dollars per month and up, depending on location.
