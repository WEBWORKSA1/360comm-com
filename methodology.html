How We Score: The 360 Score Methodology

Transparent weights. Dated prices. No pay-for-rank.

The 360 Score (0–10) is an editorial rating built from six weighted categories:

CategoryWeightWhat we look at
Pricing & value25%Entry price, all-in cost, contract flexibility, minimum seats
Features25%Calling, SMS, video, chat, contact center, AI, integrations
Reliability & security15%Uptime SLA, compliance attestations, encryption, admin controls
Ease of use15%Setup, admin portal, app quality
Support10%Channels, hours, onboarding resources
User sentiment10%Aggregated public review-site ratings

Pricing verification

We record each provider's public starting price and the date checked (Sep 30, 2026 for the current dataset). Where a vendor page could not be accessed, we cite a reputable third-party pricing guide and label it. Always confirm current pricing with the vendor.

Independence

Commercial relationships (affiliate, referral or sponsorship) never affect scores. See how we make money.
