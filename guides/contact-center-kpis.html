15 Contact Center KPIs That Actually Matter (with Benchmarks to Aim For)

Service level, ASA, AHT, FCR, occupancy and more — definitions, formulas and what 'good' looks like.

360360Comm Editorial Team · Updated Sep 30, 2026 · 8 min read

Contact centers can measure hundreds of things. These 15 metrics explain almost all customer experience and cost outcomes.

KPIFormulaCommon target
Service level% answered within X sec80% in 20 sec (voice)
Average speed of answerTotal wait ÷ answered callsUnder 30 sec
Abandonment rateAbandoned ÷ offeredUnder 5–8%
Average handle time(Talk + hold + wrap) ÷ callsVaries by industry
First contact resolutionResolved first time ÷ total70%+
OccupancyHandle time ÷ available time75–85%
ShrinkageUnavailable paid time ÷ paid timeOften 25–35%
CSATSatisfied responses ÷ total80%+
NPS% promoters − % detractorsPositive and rising
Customer effort scoreAvg ease ratingLow effort
Schedule adherenceTime in adherence ÷ scheduled90%+
Transfer rateTransferred ÷ handledLow and falling
Cost per contactTotal cost ÷ contactsTrend down
Agent attritionLeavers ÷ avg headcountBelow industry avg
Self-service containmentResolved in IVR/bot ÷ totalRising without hurting CSAT

Benchmarks vary widely by industry and channel; use targets as starting points. To translate service-level targets into headcount, use the Erlang C calculator.
